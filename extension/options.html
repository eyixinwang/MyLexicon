MYLEXICON CAPTURE

Keep your reading rhythm.

Select English text, then right-click and choose Explain in Chinese · MyLexicon, click the extension icon, or press Alt + Shift + L (Option + Shift + L on Mac).

Use the hosted MyLexicon address, or http://127.0.0.1:5173/ while developing locally.

Set up Gemini in MyLexicon Settings and open your local library there. Explanations stay temporary until you choose 加入 Revisit. Revisit entries sync and export with your existing library.

Only deliberately submitted selections and a short surrounding passage go to Gemini. This extension stores the service address; your API key stays in MyLexicon. Browser internal pages and some embedded documents cannot be captured.

You can change the shortcut on your browser’s extension keyboard-shortcuts page.
